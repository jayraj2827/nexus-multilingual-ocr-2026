Supported Formats & Ingestion

NexusOCR accepts all standard document, tabular, and image formats natively:

PDF (.pdf) PNG (.png) JPEG (.jpg) TIFF (.tiff) Word (.docx) Excel (.xlsx) CSV (.csv) PowerPoint (.pptx) WebP (.webp) HTML / Text

Audio and video media are strictly filtered at ingestion to guarantee dedicated OCR throughput.
